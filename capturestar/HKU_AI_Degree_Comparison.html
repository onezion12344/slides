AI Course Pathways

BASc(AppliedAI) — Statistical & Applied Path

ASAI1001 AI Philosophy→ SDST2601-02 Prob & Stats→ MATH3900 Optimization→ SDST3612 Stat ML→ COMP3340 Deep Learning

Statistical foundations, applied AI, 5 industry concentrations. Python-only. 100% CA in NLP & Stat ML.

BA&BEng / BEng — Computer Science Path

COMP3270 Intro to AI→ COMP2113 C/C++ & Linux→ COMP3314 ML→ COMP3340 Deep Learning→ Electives

Algorithmic CS foundations, engineering rigor, formal AI. Stronger math proofs (MATH2012). C/C++ + Java.

Shared core: COMP2119 (DSA), MATH1013, MATH2014, SDST2601-2602, COMP3340 (Deep Learning)

BASc Exclusive: 5 Concentration Tracks

Must complete ≥1 concentration (18cr). Recommended: AI Technology + optional second track.

AI Technology
Computer Graphics, Robotics, CV, NLP, HPC, Parallel Programming
AI in Business & Finance
E-Commerce, Operations Research, Financial Calculus, Marketing Analytics, Time Series
AI in Medicine
Survival Analysis, Bayesian Learning, Biostatistics, Omics Data, Medical Image
AI in Smart City
Urban Development, GIS, Remote Sensing, Environmental Studies, Transport
AI in Neurocognitive Sci
Intro Psychology, Cognitive Psychology, Perception, Cognitive Science Foundations

Who Should Choose What?

BASc(AppliedAI)

I want to apply AI to a specific industry (finance, medicine, urban planning). I prefer statistical thinking over engineering depth. I want maximum elective freedom to explore.

BA&BEng(AI&DataSc)

I want BOTH deep CS engineering AND a full humanities education. I see AI as a human challenge, not just a technical one. I'm ready for a 5-year, 300cr commitment.

BEng(AI&DataSc)

I want pure engineering depth — strong CS, strong math, strong AI. No humanities requirement. Rigorous C/C++ programming and formal methods. Focused single degree.
